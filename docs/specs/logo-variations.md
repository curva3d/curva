# Brief: logo variations

**Status:** Brief agreed in chat · 12 November 2026. Last in the order of work (decision 0016). The owner
chooses from the variations; nothing replaces the current icon until then.
**Covers:** feedback item 26 (`docs/specs/feedback-2026-11-12.md`).

## What the owner asked for

- **Centre the C.** Today the arc's ends and its left point make it sit off centre in the square.
- **Points and lines in the accent orange `#e0782f`** (decision 0016), not yellow.
- **Richer, more professional,** with Serpentine3D's mark as the reference for that quality.
- **Two directions, a few variations of each:**
  1. **Serpentine3D in spirit:** a confident mark, with depth from weight, a gentle gradient or a second
     tone, still a C made from a curve and its control points.
  2. **Construction lines:** thin geometric guides behind (the grid, circles and diagonals of an icon
     template, like the owner's second picture), and over them a much thicker C.

## What will be shown

Six to eight variations in one sheet, each at 1024, 128 and 32 pixels, in dark and light, so legibility at
small sizes can be judged. The chosen one becomes `packaging/icons/curva.svg`, with everything made from it
redone (`STATUS.md` lists where the icon is used).
