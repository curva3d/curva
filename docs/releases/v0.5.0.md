**Curva 0.5.0, preview.** Drafting: sheets with live views of the model, dimensions, text, hatches,
section styles and plans drawn from the model, printed to PDF and exchanged with Rhino, AutoCAD (DXF
and DWG) and SVG. The 3D views are now drawn by Bevy, and meshes and SubD have arrived. Still early:
expect rough edges, and keep copies of anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.5.0-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.5.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | `curva-v0.5.0-macos-arm64.dmg`: open it and drag Curva to Applications |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **macOS:** the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".

If you have 0.4.2, Curva offers this update in the command bar when it opens.

## New since 0.4.2

**Sheets and drawings**
- **Layouts**: sheets in tabs (Layout, CopyLayout), with **detail views** showing the model live, each
  with its own display mode, lock and hidden layers.
- **Title blocks, scale bars, a sheet index and revisions**, with fields that fill themselves in from
  the document's and the layout's user text. A Notes panel.
- **Print and ExportPDF** send layouts to the printer or a PDF; **SVG export** writes them as drawings.
- **Make2D** draws exact hidden lines from the solids: four-view drawings (third or first angle),
  clipping planes cutting the drawing with section fills, annotation copied across, GroupOutput and
  MaintainSourceLayers. A plan of the test house builds in about 0.2 s.

**Annotation**
- **Text** with annotation styles; **leaders**, **dots** and **hatches** (with patterns loaded from
  `.pat` files, and SetHatchBasePoint).
- **Dimensions**: linear (Dim, DimAligned, DimRotated), radius, diameter, angle, ordinate, arc length,
  curve length, area and volume.
- **Dimensions follow what they measure**: a dimension snapped to an object's end, middle or centre
  moves with it when the object changes.
- **Section styles** on objects, layers and clipping planes, used by section fills in views and in
  Make2D.
- Linetypes, sublayers, and objects' own colours.

**Files**
- **DXF and DWG**, both ways: curves, points, layers, text, hatches, blocks, ellipses, dimensions,
  leaders and multileaders, annotation styles, layouts with their viewports, and user text.
- **Rhino files (.3dm)** now also carry dimensions, leaders, text and notes, hatches, dots, layouts
  and details, clipping planes, linetypes, section styles, meshes and SubD.
- **Meshes**: STL, OBJ and PLY read and written.

**Meshes and SubD**
- Mesh objects and mesh primitives; Mesh from NURBS with a density slider; ReduceMesh,
  TriangulateMesh, QuadrangulateMesh, ExtractMeshFaces, MeshToNURB; Check and MeshRepair; mesh
  booleans.
- SubD objects: primitives' cages, editing the cage with the surface following live, ToSubD from
  meshes, and ToNURBS from SubD.

**The display**
- **Bevy draws every view**, with each view's own look, clipping planes, and Curva's lines on top.
- Large models: copies share one mesh (the *Large* test scene stays under 1 GB of graphics memory),
  snapping searches only what could win, selecting costs under 1 ms a frame, and models far from the
  origin draw without shimmering.
- Outlines of curved shapes in shaded views.

**Fixed**
- A plan no longer draws solids that lie wholly above its clipping plane.
- Selecting no longer freezes Curva on large models.
- Damaged Rhino files are refused with a message, never a crash (now checked by tests).
- Owner feedback on 0.4.2: the system's own file dialogs, the Linux icon, units kept from the file,
  the top bar, the Layers panel's columns, and names.

## Known gaps

- Plans of very large models are slow: the *Large* test scene (10,000 solids) does not yet finish.
- Shadows in drawings, and Make2D fills behind other objects, are still to come.
- In shaded views, curves hidden behind a solid's faces cannot be snapped to; switch to Ghosted or
  Wireframe to reach them.
