**Curva 0.1.7, preview.** Many more commands now work as they do in Rhino 8, each measured against
it: construction planes, sweeps, matching and blending, surface editing, clipping planes and
layouts. Display styles you can edit live, Rhino 8's mesh outlines, and snaps to exact edges. Still
early: expect rough edges, and keep copies of anything you care about.

## New since 0.1.6

**Construction planes and views, as in Rhino 8**
- **CPlane** offers Elevation, Rotate, View, 3Point, Surface (on a picked face), Through and Curve,
  and Undo and Redo step through each viewport's own planes.
- **Zoom** offers All and Factor. **NamedView** and **NamedCPlane** offer Rename and List.

**Surfaces**
- **Patch** fills any boundary with one fitted, trimmed surface.
- **MatchSrf** moves only the matched edge and defaults to Curvature. **ExtendSrf** Smooth carries a
  plain surface on smoothly.
- **RebuildUV** rebuilds one direction and keeps spheres whole. **Reparameterize** takes surfaces
  and offers the current range and Automatic.
- **FilletSrf** and **ChamferSrf** trim both surfaces back to the fillet.
- **Untrim** gives a split piece its whole plane back; **ShrinkTrimmedSrf** leaves Rhino's margin.
- **Dir** shows which way surfaces face and **Flip** turns them over. **PointsOn** shows a surface's
  control points, and moving them reshapes it.
- **UnrollSrf** lays out every face, a cylinder's side as a true rectangle. **BooleanSplit** splits
  by a surface.

**Curves**
- **Sweep1** and **Sweep2** take several sections, sections anywhere along the rails, and mitred
  corners; Sweep1 takes a degree-1 rail as a polyline.
- **Match** changes the end you pick, meets the target's end you pick, and matches curvature by
  default. **BlendCrv** blends from the ends you pick.
- **Rebuild** makes Rhino's curve: uniform knots, end tangents kept, fitted along the length.
- **RemoveKnot**, **SimplifyCrv**, **Flow** (keeping length and the original), **ArrayCrv** (copies
  turn with the curve), **Taper** (distances from the axis), and conics from the focus.

**Layouts and clipping planes**
- **Layout** names sheets Page 1, Page 2… and starts each with a detail.
- **ClippingPlane** keeps the side Rhino keeps, cuts only the viewport it was drawn in, and offers
  Vertical, 3Point and FlipDirection.

**Meshes and SubD, as in Rhino 8**
- **Mesh** meshes a lone untrimmed surface in quads, and untrimmed faces mesh as quad grids.
- Mesh booleans keep their points unwelded along hard edges and the cut; **FillMeshHoles** gives
  the filling its own points; mesh primitives are unwelded at hard edges.
- **MultiPipe** on curved curves and rings in space, with Rhino's junctions; **Bridge** Straightness;
  **ToSubD** on cones; a SubD's extent comes from its smooth surface.

**Snaps and picking**
- **Knot**, **Vertex**, **Tan** and **Project** snaps, as in Rhino 8's Osnap.
- Edges of surfaces and solids snap at their real ends, middle and centre, not at every drawn
  point; NURBS circles and arcs snap at their centre.
- Snaps remember the object and place they landed on, so dimensions follow what they were snapped
  to.
- Right-click is Enter. **SelColor**, **SelName** and **SelBlockInstanceNamed** take wildcards and
  keep what you picked; **UnlockSelected** picks among locked objects only; **Group** takes a
  single object; **Purge** offers Rhino's options and removes every unused table.

**Display**
- **Display styles you edit live** in the Style panel: lines can thin and fade with distance, be
  dashed, dotted or see-through, and outlines draw at the style's width. Styles are saved in the
  model and in My styles, and each view remembers its style by name.
- **Silhouettes** of curved surfaces in Wireframe and Ghosted, as in Rhino 8: a cylinder's sides
  now show.
- **Meshes draw Rhino 8's outline:** open borders and unwelded edges in every mode. Hidden and
  Technical show no mesh wires, and wires no longer break into dashes on slanted faces.
- **Faster mesh outlines:** on big meshes they build about 7 times faster.
- A curve lying on a surface's edge shows over it, and a selected line wins over others in the same
  place.
- **Layouts:** a layout's own objects (title block, text, lines) show over its details, and the
  paper has no grid, as in Rhino.
- **The gumball's scale squares** are back, sitting on the selection's box.

**Fixed**
- The first solid preview no longer freezes Curva for seconds on Windows.
- Switching from a layout to the Model no longer crashes, and takes one click.
- The Layers table fits its panel; long coordinates no longer overlap "Layer".
- ChamferEdge cuts its corners flat.

## Under the hood

- **solidcore**, checked before release as always: Curva stays on `9342f32` for this update. The
  latest solidcore runs every call on a kernel of its own, which will let Curva drop its patched
  copy, and could take over the loft to a point, hidden lines, hatch clipping and capping. Moving
  to it is the first work after 0.1.7 (`docs/kernel/custom-operations.md`).
- **Bevy:** 0.20.1 is still the latest release, so Curva's four custom parts (the host, CAD lines,
  clipping planes and the far origin) are all still needed.

## Not in this update

Still to come: the Layouts, Views and Print panels as planned with the owner (views in your own
folders, clone folders that make one view and one sheet per level), shortcut and mouse profiles
for Rhino, SketchUp, Archicad, Revit, Vectorworks, AutoCAD, Blender, Fusion 360 and SolidWorks,
and more commands checked against Rhino 8.

## Known gaps

- The release checks were tried by hand in the running app on Windows, except orbiting the view while Box previews and closing and restoring a panel, which the owner is trying after release. Please report anything that feels off.
- Typing a command very quickly just after a click can occasionally lose a key (#810).
