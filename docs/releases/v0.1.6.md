**Curva 0.1.6, preview.** A new interface, built on one UI library and Apple's design guidelines;
SubD and mesh modelling that follows Rhino 8, measured against it; display modes that work like
Rhino's; and a new black-and-white app icon. Still early: expect rough edges, and keep copies of
anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.1.6-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.1.6-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | `curva-v0.1.6-macos-arm64.dmg`: open it and drag Curva to Applications |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **macOS:** the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".

**Install this one by hand:** copies of 0.1.5 and earlier were built before the releases were
renumbered and still think of themselves as 0.4.x, so they will not offer 0.1.6 as an update. From
0.1.6 on, Curva offers each new update in the command bar when it opens.

## New since 0.1.5

**A new interface**
- **Frosted glass:** deep black frosted panels in dark mode and white ones in light mode, over a warm
  grey or Apple-grey background; each panel keeps its own blur, and solid panels are a choice in
  Settings.
- **A menu bar:** File, Edit, View and Help, with their keys shown; Ctrl+, opens Settings.
- **Commands in groups on one row:** the groups as a segmented control, the buttons in groups with
  thin dividers, and what does not fit in a More menu, so the row never scrolls sideways.
- **A status panel** at the bottom left, beside the command bar: where the pointer is, the unit and
  the current layer (change it there), then Ortho, Planar, Grid, SmartTrack, Gumball and Filter.
- **Chosen things** show as a soft orange wash with a thin edge, not a full orange fill.
- **Dialogs** dim the window behind them: Return gives the main answer, Esc cancels. Delete buttons
  are red. Closing a file no longer asks; the command history says what was closed. Errors show in
  red there.
- **Panels:** their hide and close marks show only while the pointer is over them; resizing the
  command bar shows more or less history and nothing else moves.
- **Settings as a form:** segmented controls instead of radio buttons, Interface size from 100% to
  200% (also Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus and Ctrl+0), Increase contrast and Reduce motion.
- **Layers panel as Rhino's:** a toolbar along the top, a search box, tighter columns, and the layer
  of what is selected softly lit.
- **Rhino's selection filter**, from the Filter item.

**Snaps, as Rhino 8**
- **Point** and **Quad** snaps, an **Osnap** switch for all object snaps at once, a **SmartTrack**
  switch, and **Planar**, which keeps picked points level with the last one.
- Guideline tracking shows a dot on the guideline rather than "Near".

**SubD and mesh modelling, measured against Rhino 8**
- **SubD:** Bevel (a strip of quads with capped ends; joined edges as one run; KeepCreases), Bridge, InsertEdge
  (with Ring and Range), InsertPoint, MergeFaces, OffsetSubD, Stitch (also across two SubDs),
  SubDLoft for closed curves and polylines, and Crease, RemoveCrease, Fill, UnweldEdge and WeldEdge.
- **MultiPipe** for straight lines and polylines.
- **Mesh:** Bevel for mesh edges, UnweldEdge, Join and Explode, and Fill for mesh holes.
- **Editing parts:** Ctrl+Shift picks parts of objects as in Rhino; picked mesh and SubD parts are
  highlighted; the gumball and Move drag picked mesh points and SubD cage points; PointsOn shows a
  mesh's points and a SubD's cage.
- **ToNURBS** makes exact NURBS from lines, polylines, circles, arcs and joined curves, as Rhino does.

**More commands as in Rhino**
- Box's Diagonal, 3Point and Vertical; Sphere 3Point and 4Point; Cylinder and Cone 2Point and 3Point;
  Torus 2Point, 3Point and Diameter.
- Arc, Ellipse, Polygon, ExtrudeCrvTapered, Revolve, PlanarSrf, Pipe, Shear, Join, Explode,
  ArrayPolar, Loft and BooleanUnion compared with Rhino 8 and matching.
- Fill and Stitch ask their choice after the picks; MeshSphere offers UV, Triangle and Quad and
  remembers Subdivisions for each.
- Tapping Alt during a gumball drag makes a copy, in one undo step.

**Display**
- **Display modes as styles:** one Style setting replaces five switches; Hidden, Technical and
  Massing modes join Wireframe, Shaded and Ghosted. Each kind of line draws as its style says.
- Smooth edges on lines, curves, solid edges and outlines; objects draw and print in their own
  colours and the document's linetypes.
- A less dense grid spaced for the unit, as Rhino's templates, with a grid colour for each mode.
- **Faster:** very large scenes draw their faces again, and a scene of 15.7 million mesh triangles
  draws in about half the time it took.

**Rhino files**
- An opened .3dm shows the views it was saved with, and its hatches draw with the file's own
  patterns. Layout details keep their scale and their Back, Left and Bottom views.

## Not in this update

the Layers table's columns, shortcut and mouse profiles, and more commands checked against Rhino 8.
