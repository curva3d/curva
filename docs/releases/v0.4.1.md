**Curva 0.4.1, preview.** Line weights in millimetres, every Rhino NURBS command the geometry engine
can do, exact geometry where 0.4.0 approximated, and no console window on Windows. Still early: expect
rough edges, and keep copies of anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.1-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |

If you have 0.4.0, Curva offers this update in the command bar when it opens.

## New since 0.4.0

**Line weights**
- Layers and objects have a **print width** in millimetres, as Rhino's Print Width: set it in the
  Layers panel (the Width column) or in Properties, from Rhino's list (0.13 to 2 mm) or typed. Objects
  follow their layer unless set. Every view shows the widths, at the screen's real size.
- Lines are now drawn once, as thin strips with their own width, instead of three times side by side:
  as fast as before at any width.

**Commands: 31 more of Rhino's**
- Curves: HandleCurve, Sketch (draw with the mouse button held), Conic, Hyperbola, BlendCrv,
  OffsetMultiple, DupBorder, Extend (Line, Arc or Smooth, to boundaries or by a length), Fair,
  CurveBoolean, Match, SimplifyCrv, Reparameterize, MakeUniform, Weight.
- Surfaces and solids: ExtrudeCrvTapered, ExtrudeCrvAlongCrv, ExtrudeCrvToPoint, RailRevolve, Pyramid,
  ExtractSrf, MergeAllFaces, Wirecut.
- Organising and views: Distribute, SelColor, Lock, Unlock, Group, Ungroup, ShowEdges, Plan.
- Every one has an icon and a place in the Commands panel.

**Exact where it was approximate**
- ExtrudeSrf pulls surfaces straight out of the construction plane, as Rhino does, curved ones too
  (it used to thicken them along their normal). The gumball's extrude circle does the same.
- DupEdge copies the exact edge picked: a line, arc or NURBS curve, not the drawn polyline.
- ChangeDegree raises the degree without changing the shape, and closed curves stay closed.
- VolumeCentroid and AreaCentroid (for surfaces) are worked out from the exact geometry.
- Parabola and Paraboloid are exact.
- `docs/commands/rhino-parity.md` notes, for every command, how it still differs from Rhino.

**Windows**
- No console window opens beside Curva.

## Known gaps

- No Rhino (.3dm) import or export yet.
- 34 of Rhino's surface tools (Sweep2, NetworkSrf, EdgeSrf, Patch, BlendSrf, surface trimming and
  others) wait on the geometry engine.
- In shaded views, curves hidden behind a solid's faces cannot be snapped to; switch to Ghosted or
  Wireframe to reach them.
