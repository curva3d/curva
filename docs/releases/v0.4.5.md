**Curva 0.4.5, preview.** Commands follow Rhino more closely, checked against Rhino 8 itself; large
Rhino files open much faster; and Curva no longer looks frozen while it works. Still early: expect
rough edges, and keep copies of anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.5-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.5-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | `curva-v0.4.5-macos-arm64.dmg`: open it and drag Curva to Applications |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **macOS:** the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".

If you have 0.4.4, Curva offers this update in the command bar when it opens.

## New since 0.4.4

**Commands that follow Rhino**
- **Checked against Rhino 8:** a new test runs 35 cases through real Rhino 8 and through Curva with
  the same typed inputs and compares what each makes; all match.
- **A typed size holds:** a distance, angle or size typed in the command line holds the preview
  at once, before Enter; the mouse only gives the direction, and a click places it at that size.
- **Box and Rectangle** take a typed length at the second step, then the width (Enter: the
  length), then the height (Enter: the width), as in Rhino; so do MeshBox, SubDBox, MeshPlane and
  SubDPlane.
- **Align** works as in Rhino 8: Bottom, Concentric, HorizCenter, Left, Right, Top and VertCenter,
  then the alignment point (Enter aligns to the selection itself); blocks align too.
- **Offset:** BothSides makes one copy on each side of curved lines, and Rhino's Cap (None, Flat,
  Round) closes the two copies of an open curve.
- **Heights follow the mouse** in Box and every solid, from the corner just clicked.
- **Scale1D and Scale2D** scale solids and blocks, not only curves.
- **Shift** on a rectangle's or box's opposite corner makes a square.
- **ToSubD** takes surfaces and polysurfaces and matches Rhino 8 on spheres, boxes and trimmed faces;
  **ToNURBS** packs faces as Rhino does.

**Previews for everything**
- Every command that makes, moves, copies or edits geometry previews whatever it acts on: blocks
  show what they contain, solids show their faces, meshes and SubDs show too.

**Selecting**
- Picking objects says what was selected, as Rhino does: "3 open curves, 1 closed polysurface added
  to selection."
- A click where several objects overlap opens Rhino's selection menu; Tab moves through them.
- The pointer snaps along the guidelines from held points, as Rhino's SmartTrack does.

**Never looks frozen**
- Opening and importing a file happen in the background; the command bar shows a progress line
  and a chip with the step and how far along it is, with a Stop button (Escape stops too).
- **Large Rhino files open much faster:** the biggest polysurfaces are read at once and healed in the
  background, each taking its place as it is ready. On a 483 MB file, building its solids took about
  100 seconds; the largest are now read in a fraction of a second each (the whole opening not yet
  timed in the app).
- Selecting a large solid no longer freezes Curva while it is measured, and the print panel no longer
  slows the viewport.

**Command line and panels**
- A step's options sit on a row of their own above the prompt.
- The specialisation toggle shows all four specialisations side by side, the chosen one lit.
- Enter never ticks a snap by mistake; the command suggestions can be scrolled with the wheel.
- Settings and the panels a command opens close without asking; a panel opened from Settings goes
  beside it. The gumball's value box takes typing.
- **Gumball:** Shift snaps a turn to every 5 degrees.
- **Tab** with nothing typed switches every SubD between smooth and flat.

**Views**
- Zooming with the wheel goes towards the point under the mouse, as in Rhino.
- A new view picker shows where each view looks from.
- Meshes show their edges in Shaded and Ghosted as well as Wireframe; smooth SubDs show their cage
  edges; SubD faces coloured one by one show their colours.
- The Display panel fits "Perspective" and resizes properly; in focus mode the Operations bar reaches
  the screen edge. A newly opened file is shown whole in every viewport.

## Not in this update

Still to come: the Layouts and Views panels and the reorganised print panel (plans awaiting the
owner's decisions), shortcut and mouse profiles, reading Rhino files on several cores, the gumball's
stretch on closed curves (being looked into), Sphere's Diameter at its first step, and more commands
checked against Rhino 8.

## Known gaps

- Nothing here has been tried by hand in the running app beyond the automated checks on Windows,
  macOS and Linux; please report anything that feels off.
- A few objects in very large Rhino files are still left out and listed in the report: a hatch whose
  boundary has a gap, and a sliver face the geometry engine refuses.
