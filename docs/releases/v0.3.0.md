**Curva 0.3.0, preview.** A new look, panels you can arrange freely, a gumball like Rhino's, and
commands that follow Rhino's steps and options. Still early: expect rough edges, and keep copies of
anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.3.0-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights. Adds Curva to the Start menu, and can be removed from Settings → Apps |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.3.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | `curva-v0.3.0-macos-arm64.dmg`: open it and drag Curva to Applications |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **macOS:** the first time, macOS will refuse to open Curva. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security
  and choose "Open Anyway" next to Curva.

## New since 0.2.0

**The look**
- Light and dark themes in warm greys, following the system or chosen in Settings, with one accent
  orange for selections, previews and focus. The interface is set in Inter.
- Every panel is frosted glass, or solid if you prefer (Settings).
- A finer grid: a faint line every unit and a stronger one every 10.
- A **File** tab: New, Open, Save, Save As, Settings and About, with Ctrl+N, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S and
  Ctrl+Shift+S.
- Command buttons show icons, with the name on hover.
- Models are in millimetres or metres (Settings); changing the unit asks whether to scale the model.

**Panels**
- Each panel shows its name along its top; drag the name to move it. A V hides it to a tab on its edge.
- Drop one panel on another to merge them as tabs; drag a tab out to make it a panel of its own.
- Floating panels settle on a grid, and every panel resizes from its edges and corners.
- A compact command bar centred at the bottom, and the agent connection in its own panel.

**Picking, snapping and undo**
- Click anywhere on a face to select its surface or solid.
- In shaded views, snap points hidden behind faces are no longer offered.
- Rest on a snap point for half a second to hold it: dashed guidelines then give snap points where
  they cross each other and objects, and midway between held points.
- Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z work even while typing in the command line, and undo brings the
  selection back too.
- `UndoView` and `RedoView` (Home and End) step through each viewport's recent views.
- Ctrl + right-drag zooms.

**The gumball, like Rhino's**
- Arrows to move, circles to extrude (curves into surfaces, flat surfaces into solids), quarter arcs to
  turn, squares to scale along an axis (Shift scales evenly), plane squares to move in a plane, and the
  centre to move freely. Drags snap and show their distance, angle or factor; click a handle without
  dragging to type an exact value.

**Commands**
- The Curves, Operations, Surfaces and Solids tabs now follow Rhino's steps and options, for example
  Circle `2Point`, `3Point` and `Diameter`, Rectangle `Center` and `3Point`, Polyline `Close` and
  `Undo`, Offset `ThroughPoint` and `BothSides`, Copy `InPlace`, Mirror `XAxis` and `YAxis`, the
  Booleans' `DeleteInput`, Revolve `FullCircle`, and Pipe `Diameter`.
- Solid previews show ghosted in orange while a command runs.
- `SaveAs`.

## Known gaps

- No Rhino (.3dm) import or export yet.
- `Cap` is not available: the geometry engine leaves capped solids hollow (reported upstream as
  tangency-labs/solidcore#3266).
- On Windows a console window opens beside Curva.
- Alt+Z may be taken by the NVIDIA overlay; Home and End do the same.
