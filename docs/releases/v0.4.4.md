**Curva 0.4.4, preview.** Meshes and SubD grow up, files open faster and more completely, and the
commands are arranged by kind of geometry: NURBS, Mesh and SubD. Still early: expect rough edges,
and keep copies of anything you care about.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.4-windows-x64-setup.exe`: an installer for your user only, with no administrator rights |
| Linux (64-bit) | `curva-v0.4.4-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it |
| macOS (Apple silicon) | `curva-v0.4.4-macos-arm64.dmg`: open it and drag Curva to Applications |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet:
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- **macOS:** the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".

If you have 0.4.3, Curva offers this update in the command bar when it opens.

## New since 0.4.3

**Commands arranged by kind of geometry**
- The top bar's tabs now start with **NURBS** (Curves, Surfaces, Solids), then **Mesh** and
  **SubD**, then the tabs every kind of geometry shares: **Edit**, **Organise**, **Annotate**,
  **Layouts**, Analyze and Select. Modify is gone; its buttons are in Edit, Organise and Solids.
  Mesh commands, text, dimensions, hatches, layouts and printing now have buttons.

**Meshes**
- Meshes show, shaded and with their vertex colours, and can be picked; large scanned meshes open
  in about half the time, and a click on one takes under 1 ms.
- **Mesh primitives:** MeshBox, MeshPlane, MeshSphere (with 2Point), MeshCylinder, MeshCone and
  MeshTorus.
- **The Mesh command** and mesh export share a **mesh settings panel**: presets, a density slider,
  and the face count, accuracy and file size in plain numbers. A mesh made from a surface or solid
  can follow it with History.
- **Mesh booleans:** MeshBooleanUnion, MeshBooleanDifference, MeshBooleanIntersection and
  MeshBooleanSplit.
- **Repair:** Check and MeshRepair, plus Rhino's single repairs.
- **Editing:** Join and Explode for meshes, ReduceMesh, TriangulateMesh, QuadrangulateMesh,
  ExtractMeshFaces and MeshToNURB, all matched to Rhino 8.
- **QuadRemesh:** keeps hard edges, with TargetEdgeLength and Symmetry (adaptive size and guide
  curves are still to come).
- Meshes and SubDs move, stretch, deform and sit in stretched blocks.

**SubD**
- SubDs show: a smooth surface with its creases and open borders, or the flat cage when set to
  flat; in Wireframe a smooth SubD shows its cage's edges on its surface, as Rhino does. They can be
  picked and print.
- **SubD primitives** on the new SubD tab: SubDBox, SubDPlane, SubDSphere, SubDCylinder, SubDCone and
  SubDTorus, with Rhino 8's coarse defaults.
- WeldEdge and UnweldEdge.
- **ToSubD** turns meshes into SubDs (surfaces and polysurfaces are not taken yet); **ToNURBS**
  turns SubDs into polysurfaces; **SubDDisplayToggle** switches every SubD between smooth and
  flat.

**Files**
- **SketchUp files open**, and Import offers them. Rhino 8's **SketchUp Import Options** dialog
  comes first: faces as **trimmed planes** (flat surfaces built from each face, joined into solids
  where groups close) or as meshes, joining edges and faces, and group choices. "Always use these
  settings" skips the dialog.
- **STEP export** of surfaces, polysurfaces and solids.
- **STL, OBJ and PLY** in Open, Import and Export; exporting asks for the mesh settings in the mesh
  panel.
- **More Rhino files come in whole:** surfaces of revolution, solids with sliver flat faces, solids
  where three faces meet at an edge, loosely joined solids, weighted sum surfaces, and capped
  extrusions far from the origin. Lines of zero length are named in the report.

**Speed**
- **Big drawings open 2 to 8 times faster:** solids, plan cuts and hidden lines are worked on every
  core. The largest test file opens in about 6 minutes instead of 15.
- The *Large* plan builds in 26 s instead of 122 s.
- Snapping stays quick with a whole large model in view: under 1 ms a frame, down from about 1.2 ms.
- Edits in a drawing full of dimensions and hatches no longer pause (about 0.4 s became 0.04 s), nor
  in a drawing full of blocks: unchanged blocks keep what they drew.
- A drawing full of text opens about 19 s sooner.
- About 1 GB less memory on the *Large* test scene: copies of a body share one mesh.

**Views**
- More camera views: Bottom, Left, Back and two-point perspective. The lens can be set in mm or
  degrees from the Display panel, and saved views keep it; Perspective uses Rhino's standard 50 mm
  lens.
- The grid shows again in 3D and flat views.

## Not in this update

0.4.4 was planned to carry every item open on 19 November; it ships now with what is ready
(decision 0039). Still to come, in the next update: live plans in the viewports, with incremental
hidden lines; dimensions that follow what they were snapped to; editing SubD cages in the viewport
and picking mesh and SubD parts; a live preview while the mesh panel is open; Technical and Hidden
views, objects' own linetypes and section patterns in views; drawing repeated objects once; and
some tidy-up of the code.

## Known gaps

- In shaded views, curves hidden behind a solid's faces cannot be snapped to; switch to Ghosted or
  Wireframe to reach them.
- A few objects in very large Rhino files are still left out and listed in the report: a hatch whose
  boundary has a gap, and a sliver face the geometry engine refuses.
