# 0022: The final logo

**Status:** Replaced by 0040 (21 November 2026). Decided by the owner in chat · 15 November 2026. Replaces the icon choice of 13 November
(the Drafting mark, `docs/specs/archive/logo-drafting-refined.md`).

**What was decided.** Curva's logo is a C drawn as an arc in plan and extruded into a solid, in warm
greys, lit from the top left. The app icon is the light version: a white rounded tile with an ink C.
There is a dark version (graphite tile, white C), versions drawn for 48, 32 and 16 px, a one-colour
version, and a lockup with the name "Curva" in Inter SemiBold. The name is always written Curva.
Orange is not in the logo; it stays the interface accent and, in brand material, marks emphasis
(underlines, highlights, short rules).

**Why.** The owner found the Drafting mark too busy. The extruded C is simple, reads as a C down to
16 px, and says what Curva does: what you draw in 2D becomes the 3D model.

**Rejected.** The Drafting mark with its construction lines and orange control points; a flat C with
a long shadow; freeform-curve, tube and twisted-ribbon versions; gradient versions in orange.
All are on the logo canvas the owner reviewed.

**Revisit if** the icon proves hard to tell apart on a platform, or the brand gains a second colour.
