# 0040: The new logo, one curve that reads C and U

**Status:** Decided by the owner in chat · 21 November 2026. Replaces 0022.

**What was decided.** Curva's logo is one closed curve inside a 50 × 50 × 50 cube, drawn as a soft,
thick tube. Seen from the front it reads **C**, for Curva; seen from above it reads **U**, for
cUrva. The owner modelled the curve in Rhino (`docs/design/brand/logo-3d/rhino/`); its corners are
filleted at radius 15 and the tube is 20 wide. It is shown from one fixed angle (turned −70°,
tilted 35°).

- **Colour:** the main logo is **amber**: the side facing the light is #F6C35B, the side in shadow
  #E0603F, and the colour also runs along the loop. A fine film grain (3.5%) gives it a matte,
  soft-touch surface. There is a **black** version for light backgrounds and a **white** one for dark
  backgrounds, both lit by the light alone.
- **App icon:** the mark alone, with no tile behind it, floating on a soft shadow. The 16 to 48 px
  sizes are drawn without grain so they stay crisp.
- **Orange is now in the logo**, "for now" in the owner's words. It is still the interface accent;
  the rules for orange in brand material (underlines, highlights, short rules) are unchanged.
- The name is still written Curva, and the lockup still uses Inter SemiBold.

**Why.** The owner wanted a logo that is truly 3D, like a sculpture that reads differently from
different angles, and one that carries the name: C from the front, U from above. The tube style
follows references he chose (soft gradient tubes, fine grain).

**Rejected.** Trip-let solids (three letters intersected); an extruded C; flat faceted and line
versions; fillet radii 20 and 25; isometric, dimetric and planometric views; a tile behind the
icon; heavy grain and stipple; grey and gloss blacks. The exploration is on the logo canvas and on the
branch `brand-logo-3d-sources`.

**Revisit if** the icon is hard to recognise at small sizes or on a platform that puts non-square
icons on a plate, or when the owner decides whether orange stays in the logo for good.
