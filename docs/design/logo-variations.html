Eight ways to draw the C

Every variant keeps the idea of the current icon: a C drawn as a curve, with its control polygon and three control points. The C is now centred in its square, and the points and polygon are in the accent orange #e0782f. Each is shown large, then at 128 and 32 pixels (and 16, for the tab) on dark and light, to judge how it reads small. Nothing replaces the current icon until one is chosen.

1 · In the spirit of Serpentine3D

A confident mark: depth from weight, a gentle gradient or a second tone.

2 · Construction lines

The guides of an icon template behind (grid, circles, diagonals), and over them a much thicker C.
